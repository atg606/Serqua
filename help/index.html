Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

What is Prollegen™?

Prollegen combines whey protein isolate with Type I & III collagen peptides, Vitamin C, MSM and DigeZyme® digestive enzymes for performance, recovery and everyday wellbeing.

What sizes are available?

Prollegen is currently listed in 500g and 1kg sizes, offering approximately 16.67 and 33.33 servings respectively.

How long does delivery take?

Typical delivery is estimated at 3–7 business days to serviceable locations across India. Final timing is shown during checkout.

When is shipping free?

Free shipping applies to orders of ₹4,499 and above.

Is cash on delivery available?

COD is available on eligible orders and serviceable locations. Eligibility will be confirmed during Shopify checkout.

How do returns and refunds work?

Please contact support with your order number before returning anything. Final eligibility will follow the published return and refund policy shown when the store launches.

How will customer accounts work?

Shopify customer accounts will use secure passwordless verification. Your profile, addresses, orders and tracking details will be managed through Shopify after integration.
