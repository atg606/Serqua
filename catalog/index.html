The anatomy of movement

The smallest movements can impact your emotional atmosphere, triggering neurochemicals to release.
Endorphins lift you, serotonin balances you, and dopamine motivates you.

Movement also increases blood flow, delivering more oxygen to the brain and body.
Even a short walk can shift your energy, focus, and sense of wellbeing.

Consistent movement regulates stress and supports a more resilient nervous system.

Movement is more than physical. It is a conversation between the body and the brain every move leaving an imprint.
