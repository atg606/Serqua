Privacy policy.

Effective: 3 September 2026

This policy explains what information we handle, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Serqua respects your privacy. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate the store, fulfil orders and provide customer support.

1. Scope and responsibility

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information handled by Serqua Private Limited through this website, customer support, marketing communications and purchases. Serqua is responsible for deciding why and how this information is used, except where another provider acts independently under its own privacy notice.

This website is currently a preview. The account forms validate entries in your browser but do not presently submit or store account details on a Serqua server. Commerce and customer-account processing will activate after Shopify integration.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Identity and contact information, such as first name, last name, email address and optional mobile number.
  • Account details and preferences, including marketing consent.
  • Order and fulfilment information, including products purchased, delivery address, billing details and order history after checkout is enabled.
  • Messages, complaints, reviews and other information you send through email, WhatsApp, social media or customer support.

Information collected automatically

Our current preview stores cart contents locally in your browser using local storage. After launch, the website and service providers may also process device, browser, IP address, referral, interaction, cookie and similar technical information needed for security, checkout, analytics and store operation.

Payment information

Payment card, banking or cash-on-delivery information may be processed by Shopify and payment providers. Serqua does not intend to store complete payment-card details on its own systems.

3. How we use personal information

Depending on the service you request and applicable law, we may use information to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process payments, orders, delivery, returns, replacements and refunds.
  • Verify transactions, prevent fraud and protect the website.
  • Respond to questions, complaints and customer-service requests.
  • Send service messages about orders or account activity.
  • Send optional product news or promotions when you have chosen to receive them.
  • Measure and improve our website, products, support and marketing.
  • Maintain business, tax and compliance records and meet legal obligations.

Where consent is required, you may withdraw it for future processing. Some information may still be needed to fulfil a contract, provide a requested service, prevent misuse or comply with law.

4. When we share information

We may share only the information reasonably needed with service providers that support our store, including:

  • Shopify for ecommerce hosting, checkout, customer accounts and order administration.
  • Payment providers, banks and fraud-prevention services.
  • Delivery, warehousing and fulfilment partners.
  • Email, messaging, customer-support, analytics and technology providers.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, regulators or authorities where required.

Some providers may process information outside India. Where cross-border processing occurs, we will use it subject to applicable legal requirements and provider safeguards. We may also transfer relevant information as part of a lawful merger, financing, restructuring or sale of the business.

We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and verification of your request, you may ask for information about processing, correction or updating of inaccurate data, deletion of data that is no longer required, withdrawal of consent, or resolution of a grievance. Certain information may need to be retained where permitted or required by law.

You can unsubscribe from promotional email using the link in the message or by contacting us. You will still receive necessary transactional or service communications.

You may clear locally stored cart information using your browser settings. When cookies or optional analytics are introduced, corresponding controls and notices should be made available where required.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including order fulfilment, support, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Retention periods vary by record type and legal requirement.

We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and the service. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect access to your email, phone and customer account and notify us if you suspect misuse.

7. Children’s privacy

Our store is intended for adults able to make purchases under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children for independent use of the store. A parent or lawful guardian should contact us if they believe a child has provided information improperly.

8. Third-party links and policy changes

Links to Shopify, payment services, delivery providers, WhatsApp, Instagram or other third parties are governed by their own privacy notices. We are not responsible for third-party practices outside our control.

We may update this policy as the store, technology, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. We will provide additional notice where a material change requires it.

9. Contact and grievances

To ask a privacy question, exercise a request or raise a grievance, contact Serqua Private Limited at carol.serqua@gmail.com or through WhatsApp. Describe your request and provide enough information for us to verify and respond to it.

Before launch, Serqua should publish the designated privacy or grievance contact’s name and business address here if required for the final operating setup.
