# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults of all genders who care about strength, energy, recovery, and sustainable everyday wellness. They want products that fit naturally into an active life without the visual noise or exclusivity of hardcore bodybuilding culture.

## Product Purpose

Serqua is a lifestyle and wellness brand that helps people build a stronger, more balanced daily routine. The homepage should establish trust, introduce the brand's flagship nutrition products, and make the path to shopping immediately clear.

## Brand Personality

Premium, disciplined, and composed. The voice is confident and direct, but inclusive rather than intimidating. The emotional goal is focused momentum: visitors should feel that wellness is a capable daily practice, not a punishing ideal.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud bodybuilding imagery, neon gym aesthetics, clinical pharmaceutical styling, generic beige wellness branding, and crowded marketplace merchandising. Do not imitate Pant Project literally; borrow its clarity, commerce rhythm, and decisive product presentation.

## Design Principles

1. Make discipline feel inviting, not severe.
2. Let real products and active lives carry the story.
3. Lead with one clear promise and one clear next action.
4. Balance editorial brand confidence with practical commerce cues.
5. Design inclusively for an all-gender wellness audience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, strong keyboard focus states, semantic landmarks, descriptive image alternatives, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable from small mobile screens through wide desktops.
